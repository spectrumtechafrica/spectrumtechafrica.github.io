Our Community

Belonging makes growth possible.

Spectrum Tech Africa is a supportive community of queer African youths who are passionate about technology, finance and building a better future.

Join Our Community  →

A Place to Learn and Belong

Our community brings together LGBTQ+ African youths aged 18–30 to learn new skills, share ideas, ask questions and build meaningful connections.

We believe people grow when they have access to knowledge, encouragement and a supportive network. Together, we are building a generation of confident, skilled and connected individuals.
