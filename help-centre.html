Getting started

What is Spectrum Tech Africa?

Spectrum Tech Africa (STA) is an inclusive learning initiative that supports young people through technology and finance education, mentorship, and career development. Our aim is to make practical learning opportunities accessible and supportive.

How do I apply for an STA course?

Complete the official application form when applications are open. Provide accurate contact details and information about your learning interests. Follow the instructions shared through STA's official communication channels.

What happens after I submit my application?

Applicants may be invited to complete a screening process. This can include an online form and a short one-to-one interview. Successful applicants receive an official admission or offer message with the next steps.

Do I need previous programming experience?

Not necessarily. Beginner courses are designed to introduce concepts step by step. Check the requirements for the specific course you are applying to.

Are STA courses free?

Course fees and scholarship opportunities depend on the specific cohort and programme. Always check the official announcement or admission offer for the applicable fee and any available scholarship.

Learning platform

How do I access my online classroom?

After admission, check the email address you used during registration for your classroom invitation and access instructions. Follow the link to join the correct Google Classroom using the account specified by STA.

Which tools does STA use for online learning?

Depending on the course, STA may use Google Classroom for course announcements and activities, Google Colab for coding practice, Google Drive for learning materials, and Google Forms for quizzes or submissions.

I cannot access Google Classroom. What should I do?
  • Confirm that you are signed in to the correct Google account.
  • Open the invitation link sent to your registered email.
  • Check that the link has not expired or been replaced.
  • If the problem continues, contact your course facilitator or STA programme coordinator.
Do I need a laptop to participate?

A laptop or desktop computer is strongly recommended for coding and practical activities. Some course materials may be accessible on a smartphone, but a phone may not support every learning task comfortably.

Courses and learning

How are STA courses organised?

Course activities may include lessons, demonstrations, guided practice, independent challenges, discussions, assignments and assessments. Your course facilitator will share the schedule and expectations for your cohort.

How do I keep up with weekly activities?

Check Google Classroom and your official learner communication channel regularly. Follow the weekly learning plan, note deadlines, complete activities in order and ask questions early when you need help.

What if I miss a live session?

Notify your facilitator if possible, review any materials or session notes shared afterwards, and ask how to catch up on missed work. Any recording or make-up arrangement will depend on the course.

How can I ask my facilitator a question?

Use the official course discussion space or the communication channel provided for your cohort. Include the course week, activity or question you are referring to so the facilitator can assist you efficiently.

Assignments and assessment

Where do I submit my assignments?

Submit assignments through the method stated in the activity instructions, usually Google Classroom or a linked Google Form or Colab notebook. Check that your submission is complete before the deadline.

Can I resubmit an assignment?

Resubmission depends on the assignment settings and course instructions. If you need another attempt or have submitted the wrong file, contact your facilitator promptly rather than creating multiple unrequested submissions.

How will my work be graded?

Assessment methods and grading criteria are shared by the facilitator. Read the rubric before starting, answer every part of the task and make sure your work demonstrates your own understanding.

What should I do if I disagree with a grade?

Review the assignment instructions and rubric first. Then contact your facilitator respectfully, identify the specific part you would like reviewed and provide a clear explanation.

Policies and conduct

What are the expectations for group work?

Every group member is expected to communicate respectfully, complete agreed tasks, contribute meaningfully and give appropriate credit. If a member is not participating, document reasonable attempts to contact them and inform the facilitator.

Can I use AI tools for assignments?

Use of AI must follow the assignment instructions and STA's Responsible AI Use Policy. Where AI assistance is permitted, use it transparently and responsibly. You remain responsible for checking, understanding and being able to explain the work you submit.

What counts as plagiarism?

Plagiarism includes presenting another person's words, code, ideas or work as your own without proper acknowledgement. Submit original work, cite sources when required and clearly disclose assistance in line with course rules.

How should learners treat one another?

STA expects respectful, inclusive and professional behaviour in every learning space. Bullying, harassment, discrimination, threats, abusive language and sharing another learner's private information without consent are not acceptable.

Certificates and support

Will I receive a certificate after the course?

Certificate eligibility depends on the requirements of the specific programme. These may include completing required activities, meeting assessment thresholds and fulfilling attendance or participation expectations. Your cohort will receive the applicable criteria.

My name is incorrect on my learner records. How can I correct it?

Contact the programme coordinator through your official cohort channel. Provide the correct name and the details needed to identify your learner record. Use the name you want printed on your certificate where requested.

How do I report a problem or make a complaint?

Contact your course facilitator or programme coordinator through the official communication channel. Explain what happened, when it happened and what support you need. Include relevant evidence where appropriate. STA aims to handle concerns fairly and with suitable confidentiality.

How can I follow STA announcements?

Follow @spectrumtechafrica on Instagram and monitor the official communication channel provided for your cohort.
